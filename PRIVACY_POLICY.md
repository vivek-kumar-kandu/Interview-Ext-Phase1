# Privacy Policy for InterviewOS

**Last Updated:** August 9, 2026

InterviewOS ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Chrome Extension ("InterviewOS") collects, uses, and safeguards user information when you use our extension.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use InterviewOS, we may process the following types of information:

- **Candidate Profile & Resume Data:** Resume files (PDF/DOCX), candidate names, technical skills, and career history uploaded directly by the user for interview evaluation and match analysis.
- **Job Posting Data:** Job titles, company names, job descriptions, and required technical skills extracted from active job posting pages (such as LinkedIn, Greenhouse, and Indeed) when you open the extension.
- **Interview & Audit Metrics:** User responses, turn scores, time elapsed, and integrity audit metrics (such as tab switches and fullscreen state) generated during mock technical interviews.

---

### 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected data strictly to fulfill the single purpose of the extension:
- To generate real-time job-to-candidate match scores and skill gap analyses.
- To conduct adaptive technical mock interviews inside the Chrome Side Panel.
- To calculate performance scores, category breakdowns, and exportable PDF report snapshots.

---

### 3. Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

- **No Sale of Data:** We do **NOT** sell, rent, trade, or transfer your personal data or uploaded resumes to third parties.
- **Approved Use Only:** Data is processed solely to provide real-time interview feedback and analysis as requested by the user.
- **No Unrelated Uses:** We do not use user data for advertising, marketing, credit scoring, or lending purposes.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

InterviewOS uses secure backend web services and AI language models (Google Gemini) to process resume parsing and turn evaluations. Data transmitted to backend endpoints is processed over encrypted HTTPS connections solely for generating response evaluations.

---

### 5. Storage & Retention

Candidate profile settings and past session report snapshots are stored locally on your device using Chrome's `chrome.storage.local` API. You may delete saved interview reports at any time directly through the extension interface.
